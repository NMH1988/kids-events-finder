(Added after review round 2, widened to the whole file after round 3; the review rule by the owner's decision of 2026-11-25.)

- [ ] Owner's rule of 2026-11-25 (no copyright infringement, no breach of any law): it never copies another product's layout, look, text, images or brand and names no existing app as a model (UrhG, DesignG, § 4 Nr. 3 and § 5 Abs. 3 Nr. 1 UWG, MarkenG), proposes no app name or logo without the 6.5 check, uses only the fonts and Material icons shipped inside Streamlit and emoji, and asks the owner when unsure - verified by `test_ui_designer_never_copies_another_product`, `test_ui_designer_uses_only_assets_shipped_inside_streamlit`.
