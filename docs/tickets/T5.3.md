### T5.3 Trip plan box from the tool results (L) · status: todo · issue #22
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/CAPSTONE_CHECKLIST.md`): interactive prototype, user interface and interaction quality, function calling (tool results shown by the app); Legal (`docs/LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md`): 4.3 (grounding: facts from tools, shown by code), 7.2 and 7.3 (no guarantee; never invent times; check bahn.de), 6.3 (source credits), register: data sources (Transitous).
**Depends on:** none
**Acceptance criteria** (drafted by Claude, approved by the owner on 2026-11-24)
- [ ] When a turn used `find_train_connection`, the app shows a box under the answer with every journey of every train search in that turn (so both the way there and the way back): date, from, to, departure, arrival, duration, transfers, rides, and the live-data, delay and cancelled notes where the result has them - verified by unit tests of a new extractor next to `calculations()` in `src/agent/runner.py` and an `AppTest`.
- [ ] When the turn used `get_weather_forecast`, the box shows each day's summary, lowest and highest temperature and rain chance from the result - verified by the same tests.
- [ ] Service text (station, line and town names) is shown as plain text, never as Markdown or HTML: a ride named `[x](https://evil.example)` or `<img src=x>` appears literally - verified by a test.
- [ ] The box says "These times come from the journey planner; if the AI's text differs, use these." (changed on 2026-11-24 after a legal-reviewer note, approved by the owner: no promise that the box is right, since Transitous gives no guarantee), ends with "Train times and fares can change: check bahn.de or the DB Navigator app before you travel.", and the existing source credits stay - verified by an `AppTest`.
- [ ] Added on 2026-11-24 (legal-reviewer note, approved by the owner; widened on 2026-11-27 by the owner after legal round 1 of PR #45): the Research step reads https://transitous.org/sources/ for every feed behind German journeys, the timetable feeds (times, stations, line names) as well as the live feeds behind the delay, live-data and cancelled notes. A feed that asks for its own credit is credited (a link to a page that lists the required details may be enough under CC BY 4.0 s. 3(a)(2); the reasoning is recorded); a feed with share-alike, non-commercial, unclear or no licence goes to the owner before the box shows its data (6.3, 6.4); each feed found gets a line in the data source register in `docs/LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md` that says whether commercial use is allowed - verified by the progress entry naming what was found, the register diff and, where a credit is needed, a credit test.
- [ ] A turn without these tools (or the ticket and budget tools added below) shows no box, and a tool result with an `error` field is left out - verified by unit tests.
- [ ] The system prompt is unchanged - verified by the PR's diff (no change under `src/prompts/`).
- [ ] (added by T5.12, approved by the owner on 2026-11-26) The box follows `docs/design/ui-design-spec.md`, "Trip plan box": a header saying the figures are calculated by the app, with the date, the route and the party; one card per journey with large departure and arrival times; the weather range as a large figure with the Open-Meteo credit right under it; the ticket comparison and the budget move into this box (from today's separate calculation box) as a ticket card headed "Prices as of <checked_on>" with today's fare warnings, and three `st.metric` figures (known costs, budget, left); the bahn.de sentence sits in an `st.info` - verified by an `AppTest` that finds each part.
- [ ] (added by T5.12, approved by the owner on 2026-11-26) The budget state follows `status` from `src/budget.py`: a green "Within budget" badge only for `within_budget`, a yellow "Within budget so far" for `within_budget_so_far` (owner decision 2026-11-26), a gray "Over budget by €x" for `over_budget`, never red - verified by an `AppTest` per status and a test that no state shows a red element.
- [ ] (added by T5.12, approved by the owner on 2026-11-26) The box also shows when a turn used only `compare_train_tickets` or `check_budget` (then just the ticket card or the budget section, with their warnings), so the app's money figures never live only in the model's text - verified by `AppTest`s for a ticket-only and a budget-only turn.
- [ ] (added by T5.12, approved by the owner on 2026-11-26) The `st.progress` of known costs over the budget is capped at 1.0 when over budget and left out when the budget is 0, so it never raises an error - verified by unit tests for an over-budget trip and a budget of 0.
- [ ] (added by T5.12, approved by the owner on 2026-11-26) The unknown-cost labels (written by the model) are plain text, never a badge or Markdown: a label `[x](https://evil.example)`, a bare `https://evil.example` or `<img src=x>` appears literally - verified by the plain-text test above, extended to these labels.
- [ ] (added on 2026-11-27, owner decision after legal round 1 and code round 1 of PR #45) The header's "calculated by the app" covers only the money figures: the ticket card and the budget section keep "If a figure in the AI's text differs from this box, this box is right." (the app's own sums from `data/fares.json` and `src/budget.py`); the journey cards carry criterion 4's journey-planner sentence and the weather card names Open-Meteo, so no time or forecast reads as calculated by the app (7.2, 7.3) - verified by an `AppTest` that finds each sentence in its part and no "calculated by the app" wording over the journey or weather cards.
- [ ] (added on 2026-11-27, owner decision after legal round 1 of PR #45) A journey without live data (`live_data` false) says "No live data" instead of showing no note, so it never reads as on time; a delay is labelled as the first train's departure delay, since `delay_min` comes from the first ride only (7.3) - verified by unit tests of the extractor and an `AppTest`.
