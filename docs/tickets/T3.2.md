### T3.2 Datenschutzerklärung and Impressum (M) · status: todo · issue #4
**Serves:** Capstone: ethical considerations; Legal: 2.3, 2.8, 7.1, 7.2, "Before a public deployment" (legal texts).
**Depends on:** T3.1 (the privacy policy names the AI provider and its transfer basis)
**Acceptance criteria** (draft by Claude, 2026-11-23; the owner approves before the ticket starts)
- [ ] A Datenschutzerklärung page lists every purpose, legal basis, processor, third-country transfer (including the embedding providers' countries) and retention period from the registers in `docs/LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md` - verified by a test that every processing-register row and every processor is named on the page.
- [ ] It also names the controller and how to contact them, the legitimate interests pursued, the recipients, the data subject rights, the right to withdraw consent, the right to complain to a supervisory authority, and whether there is automated decision-making (Art. 13 GDPR; to be confirmed by the lawyer) - verified by a test on the page text; rule 2.3 is extended to match.
- [ ] An Impressum page (§ 5 DDG, § 18 MStV) with the details the owner provides; no personal details are written by Claude, and they enter the public repository only at launch, after the lawyer has answered "Impressum without the home address?" (git history keeps them for good) - owner check.
- [ ] Terms of use: 18+, no guarantee of correctness, check official websites - verified by an AppTest of the text.
- [ ] All three are linked from every page - verified by an AppTest per page.
- [ ] A lawyer or Datenschutzberater has reviewed the texts before launch - owner check, recorded in `docs/LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md`.
**Notes:** the texts are drafts for the owner; Claude does not write legal advice. Server logs and the hosting provider have no register row yet; they come with the hosting choice.
