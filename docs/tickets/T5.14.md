### T5.14 Feedback row with an honest share state (S) · status: todo · issue #41
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/CAPSTONE_CHECKLIST.md`): user interface and interaction quality, evaluation (user feedback); Legal (`docs/LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md`): 2.2 and 2.8 (withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it), 2.7 (90 days stated), 4.2 (no pre-tick, neutral wording), 1.2 (the hint to leave family details out).
**Depends on:** T5.12
**Acceptance criteria** (drafted by Claude, approved by the owner on 2026-11-26)
- [ ] The "share" tick sits before the rating, is never pre-ticked, and its label says that the question and answer are kept on this app's server for 90 days, can be deleted while the page is open, and should be left off if they mention the family - verified by an `AppTest`.
- [ ] Once the rating is saved, the tick and the thumbs are disabled, and the row shows either "Shared with your rating." with a "Delete everything I shared" button right there, or "Not shared. Only the rating and technical facts were saved." - verified by `AppTest`s for both states.
- [ ] The inline delete button removes the shared records exactly as the end-of-chat button does (`delete_shared_feedback`), and the end-of-chat button stays - verified by a test.
- [ ] Ticking or unticking after the rating can no longer look like a change of consent: no enabled tick remains once the rating is saved - verified by the same `AppTest`s.
