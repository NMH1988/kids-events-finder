### T5.5 Source credits for data from earlier turns (M) · status: todo · issue #24
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/CAPSTONE_CHECKLIST.md`): ethical considerations (licences and attribution); Legal (`docs/LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md`): 6.3 (the app credits the live sources it used).
**Depends on:** none
**Acceptance criteria** (drafted by Claude, approved by the owner on 2026-11-24)
- [ ] Under an answer, the app credits every live source used so far in the conversation, also when this turn called no tool and reuses an earlier turn's results (crediting a source too often is allowed, missing one is not) - verified by a test: turn 1 calls `find_nearby_places`, turn 2 calls no tool, and the OpenStreetMap and geocoding credits show under both answers.
- [ ] An answer in a conversation that used no live tool shows no credit line - verified by a test.
- [ ] The credits are set by the app from the conversation's tool results, never from the model's text, and "New conversation" starts again without credits - verified by tests.
- [ ] (added by T5.12, approved by the owner on 2026-11-26) Every answer follows one order (`docs/design/ui-design-spec.md`, "Answer footer and feedback"): the "AI-generated" badge with the warning as body text and the hidden `data-ai-generated="true"` marker unchanged; the answer; the app's box; a "Sources" badge followed by the credits; the link note in an `st.info` with a neutral link icon and no ✅; "How this answer was made"; the feedback row - verified by an `AppTest` that checks the order and that the marker is still there.
- [ ] (added by T5.12, approved by the owner on 2026-11-26) The Open-Meteo credit links to https://open-meteo.com/ and to the CC BY 4.0 licence, next to the weather data it credits, as `docs/THIRD_PARTY_TERMS.md` asks; the OpenStreetMap credit links to its copyright page - verified by a test on the credit strings and an update of the "In the app today" lines in `docs/THIRD_PARTY_TERMS.md`.
